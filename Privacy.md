# PixelsMeasure Privacy Policy

Last updated November 17, 2026

This policy covers the PixelsMeasure app for Mac, iPhone, and iPad.

## Images, screen capture, and documents

PixelsMeasure processes images and measurements on your device. When you choose an image from Photos or Files, paste one from the clipboard, or open an image in the app, PixelsMeasure uses that image for measurement. On Mac, Live Measure uses macOS Screen Recording permission to capture the display you choose. You can revoke this permission in System Settings. Measuring an image file does not require Screen Recording permission.

A saved `.pixelsmeasure` document can contain the source image, measurements, and document settings. An annotated image export can also reveal the underlying image and measurements. When you copy, export, or share content, it goes to the clipboard, location, app, or recipient you select.

Documents are stored in the location you use. Where available, PixelsMeasure can use its folder in iCloud Drive as the default location for new documents. Apple stores and syncs files in iCloud Drive under your Apple Account. If you use another file provider, that provider may store and sync your files under its own terms. PixelsMeasure does not upload your images or measurement documents to servers operated by PixelsMeasure. You can manage or delete saved documents in Finder, Files, or the file provider you use. Copies you share are managed by their recipients.

## App settings, purchases, and reviews

The app stores preferences and limited usage information, such as counts and dates used to decide when to request an App Store review, on your device. PixelsMeasure does not use in-app analytics or advertising tracking.

Apple processes App Store purchases and billing. The app uses Apple’s StoreKit to check your purchase or subscription status and restore access to paid features. PixelsMeasure does not receive your payment card details. Apple handles App Store ratings and reviews. If you choose to submit a review, Apple may publish it on the App Store.

For more about how Apple handles your information, see [Apple’s Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/).

## Support, retention, and privacy requests

If you email us, we receive your email address, your message, and any attachments you send. We use that information to respond and resolve the issue. We keep support correspondence as long as needed to handle your request and related follow-up, or as required by law. We may disclose information when required by law.

You can request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information we hold by emailing [pixelsmeasure@gmail.com](mailto:pixelsmeasure@gmail.com). Use your storage provider’s controls for files it holds and Apple’s privacy controls for data held by Apple.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, please contact us so we can address it.
