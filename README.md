## How it works

On Mac, measure your screen with **Live Measure** from the menu bar. Use Probe to read gaps between edges and Cross to check alignment and pointer coordinates. Pin the measurements you want to keep, then choose **Open as Document** to continue editing them with the captured image. Live Measure requires macOS Screen Recording permission; measuring an image does not.

On Mac, iPhone, and iPad:

1. **Open a screenshot, photo, or mockup.** Zoom in to see the pixel grid and adjust edge detection tolerance when shadows or low contrast make edges hard to pick out.
2. **Pin and edit measurements.** Organize pins in layers and use the inspector to adjust their position and size.
3. **Save or share your work.** Keep the original image and measurements in an editable `.pixelsmeasure` document, export an annotated PNG, or copy measurement values as CSS, JSON, or plain text. Save the document in iCloud Drive to reopen it on your other devices.

## Free and Pro

| Free | Pro |
| --- | --- |
| Pin up to 3 measurements per document or live session | Pin unlimited measurements |
| Keep using Probe and Cross after reaching the pin limit | Measure objects and areas, and snap guides to edges |
| Save and share editable documents and export annotated PNGs | Copy multiple measurements at once, crop, and rotate images |

Try the Pro tools in the sample document included in the app.
